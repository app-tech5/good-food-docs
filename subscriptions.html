Subscriptions

How membership plans appear to customers, what benefits they unlock, and how those plans are created in admin.

Plans Benefits Subscribe

Membership in the customer app

Customer subscriptions are recurring plans (for example monthly) that unlock benefits such as free delivery, a food discount, member-only deals, or priority support. The customer app shows only plans whose target is customer.

Each plan card lists the name, price and billing cycle, the benefit bullets, and a Subscribe action. After a successful purchase path (via your configured gateways), the membership stays attached to that account until it expires or is cancelled under your rules.

How a customer subscribes

  1. Sign in with a customer account.
  2. Open the Subscriptions / membership screen from account or settings (label depends on your translations).
  3. Compare the listed plans and tap Subscribe on the one you want.
  4. Complete payment with an enabled gateway. Confirm the plan shows as active afterward.

Where plans are defined

You create and edit tiers in the admin app under Subscriptions (name, price, billing cycle, benefits, active flag, target = customer). Details and earnings impact are covered in admin monetization.
