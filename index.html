Customer app

The storefront of your marketplace — where hungry people become paying orders, then come back because the next meal feels easy.

Android & iOS Multi-restaurant End-to-end order

Who this app is for

Diners in your city. They are not here to learn a “platform” — they want food tonight, a fair price, and a clear idea of when the bag arrives. The customer app is that experience: discovery, cart, payment, and peace of mind after checkout.

If you are evaluating the suite as a buyer or agency, start here: this is the surface your end customers will judge in the first thirty seconds.

Where it sits in the suite

An order is one story told by four other products:

  • Restaurant app — accepts and cooks what the customer just paid for
  • Driver app — carries the bag and proves the drop-off
  • Admin — configures the city, money rules, and support view
  • Backend API — keeps one shared order record for everyone

When kitchen status or courier position changes, the customer should see it without calling support. That only works because every app shares the same marketplace brain — not three disconnected databases.

What “good” looks like for your brand

A first-time user finds a restaurant, builds a basket, pays with a method you actually support, and can answer “where is my food?” without leaving the app. Regulars come back through wallet balance, membership perks, and offers that feel local — not generic.
