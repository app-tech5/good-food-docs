Checkout & vouchers

Claim vouchers on the Offers surface, then confirm address and payment to place the order.

Offers Checkout

Convert the cart

After the cart looks right, customers either claim a voucher on the Offers surface or go straight to checkout.

Offers tab — vouchers to claim

The Offers surface lists claimable vouchers / campaigns (separate from the small offers block on a restaurant page). Coupon codes and campaign rules are configured in admin — see Promotions and Coupons.

Checkout — address, payment, place order

Checkout confirms fulfillment (delivery vs pickup), the address, and the payment method (card, cash on delivery, wallet, or other gateways you activate). After place order, follow progress in order history / live tracking.

  1. Confirm delivery or pickup, address, and payment method.
  2. Place the order.
  3. Open order history / tracking to follow status.
