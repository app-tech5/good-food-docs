Wallet & payments

How the in-app balance works for customers, how they top up, and what you configure so checkout and refunds stay coherent.

Balance Top up Payment methods

What the wallet is for

The wallet is a prepaid balance inside the customer app. Instead of entering a card on every order, the customer can pay from money already on the account. That shortens checkout, reduces failed payments at the door of the kitchen, and gives you a place to land refunds or promotions as credit.

On the Wallet screen the customer sees the current balance, actions to add or send money (depending on what you enable), and the list of saved payment methods (card brands, Google Pay, PayPal, cash, and similar).

How a customer uses it

  1. Open the drawer or account area and choose Wallet.
  2. Check the balance. Use the refresh control if the amount looks stale after a payment.
  3. Tap Add Money, choose an amount (or a quick chip such as $10 / $20 / $50), select a payment method, then confirm Add to balance.
  4. At checkout, choose the wallet as the payment method when the balance covers the order (or the part your rules allow).

What you configure as operator

Payment gateways and marketplace payment behaviour are managed in the admin app — see Monetization. Enable the gateways you actually use in each market before expecting top-ups to succeed in production.
