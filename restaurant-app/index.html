Restaurant app

The partner workspace for service — accept tickets, keep the menu honest, run the pass, and grow visibility without a second toolkit.

Android & iOS Service ops Kitchen Display

Who this app is for

Owners, shift managers, and kitchen staff. They need to know what just came in, what is burning, and whether the catalog customers see still matches what the kitchen can cook.

It is not a consumer storefront — it is the operational twin of the customer app for each restaurant on your marketplace.

Where it sits in the suite

When a diner places an order, this app is the first human gate:

  • Accept or reject — the kitchen commits (or declines) before a courier is wasted
  • Kitchen Display — the same order as tickets on the pass
  • Driver app — picks up when food is ready
  • Admin — commissions, sponsorship, and support if something goes wrong

Menu edits, hours, and analytics stay with the partner so you are not the bottleneck for every price change. Subscriptions and sponsored listings let restaurants invest in better economics and placement inside the customer feed.

What “good” looks like during service

New tickets are impossible to miss, the board advances New → Preparing → Ready without paper slips, 86’d items disappear from the customer menu, and owners can see bestsellers after a few real days — not empty charts on day one.

Detailed screens for orders, menu, hours, analytics, KDS, subscriptions, and sponsored campaigns live on the feature pages — this overview explains the partner role, not a second gallery of those captures.
