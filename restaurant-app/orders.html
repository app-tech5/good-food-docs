Live orders: accept and reject

Incoming orders land in the restaurant orders list in real time. Operators accept or reject, then move the meal through prepare / ready so the driver (or pickup customer) can collect it.

For a paperless board on the pass, use the Kitchen Display (KDS) — it shares the same order pipeline.

Service loop

  1. Keep the restaurant open when you can fulfill orders.
  2. Watch the orders list; accept what you can cook, reject with a clear reason when you cannot.
  3. Advance status as food progresses; mark ready for courier or pickup.
  4. Use order history when you need past tickets or disputes.
