Sponsored visibility

How a restaurant launches a paid placement campaign (search, home banner, or both) from its own app.

Campaign Bid Placement

Buy attention in the customer feed

Sponsored listings let a restaurant pay for better visibility: top of search, a home banner slot, or both. The restaurant fills a short campaign form — headline, daily bid, placement — then launches.

Launch a campaign

  1. Sign in to the restaurant app (live API mode recommended for real campaigns).
  2. Open Sponsored listings.
  3. Under New campaign, set a headline customers will recognize.
  4. Enter a daily bid amount and choose placement (search top, home banner, or search + banner).
  5. Tap Launch campaign, then confirm the campaign appears under My campaigns.
  6. Optionally verify in admin sponsored listings.
