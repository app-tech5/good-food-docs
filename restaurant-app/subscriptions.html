Subscriptions

How restaurant partners subscribe to plans that change commission, visibility, or SaaS-style access.

Restaurant plans Benefits Subscribe

Plans partners buy from their app

Restaurant subscriptions are tiers with target restaurant. Benefits often include a lower platform commission, eligibility for sponsored placement, better support, or tool access.

Restaurant steps

  1. Sign in as the restaurant user.
  2. Open Subscriptions from the drawer or settings.
  3. Review price, cycle, and benefits; tap Subscribe on the chosen plan.
  4. After activation, confirm commission / feature changes on the next orders.

Where you edit tiers

Admin → Subscriptions, target = restaurant. See Admin subscriptions.
