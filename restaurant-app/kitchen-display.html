Kitchen Display (KDS)

How the paperless kitchen board works: tickets, columns, and running it on a tablet at the pass.

Tickets Columns Tablet

What the Kitchen Display does

The Kitchen Display is a board inside the restaurant app for cooks and the pass. After an order is accepted, it appears as a ticket with items, timing cues, and actions to move it through prep.

Columns such as New, Preparing, and Ready group tickets. Age on the ticket helps the line see what is burning.

Happy path on a busy service

  1. A customer places a delivery or pickup order.
  2. Restaurant staff accept it on Live orders (or via your auto-accept rules).
  3. Open Kitchen Display from the drawer — preferably on a tablet in landscape.
  4. Advance each ticket: accept → prepare → ready.
  5. When food is ready, the driver (or pickup customer) completes the handoff.

Tablet tips

Mount the device at eye level on the pass, keep brightness high, and disable auto-lock during service. Prefer a stable Wi‑Fi path to your API.
