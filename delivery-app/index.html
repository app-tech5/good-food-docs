Driver app

The courier’s shift in one pocket — jobs, navigation, proof at the door, and a clear picture of what the day paid.

Android & iOS Last mile Proof of delivery

Who this app is for

Couriers on bikes, scooters, or cars. They work with one hand, in traffic, and they need large targets — not a desktop dashboard squeezed onto a phone. The driver app is built for that reality.

If your marketplace promises “live tracking” and “delivered with proof,” this is the product that makes those promises true on the street.

Where it sits in the suite

The driver never invents a separate order. They work the same ticket the customer paid for and the restaurant accepted:

  • Customer app — follows the same delivery on the tracking map
  • Restaurant app — hands off food when it is ready
  • Admin — sees assignment, payouts, and disputes
  • Backend API — assigns work, stores POD, updates status for everyone

Batching nearby drops, live position, and photo + signature at the door are how you cut empty miles and “I never got it” tickets.

What “good” looks like on a shift

The courier goes online, sees clear jobs, stacks nearby drops when it makes sense, finishes with evidence when rules require it, and can open earnings without guessing what the marketplace recorded.

Step-by-step screens for the list, active job, POD, earnings, and payouts are on the feature pages — this overview stays on the courier role inside your marketplace.
