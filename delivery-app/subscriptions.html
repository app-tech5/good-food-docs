How courier priority tiers appear in the driver app — and how you define those plans in admin.

Priority Benefits Subscribe

Priority plans for couriers

Driver subscriptions are tiers targeted at driver accounts. Typical benefits include priority job access, support priority, or other marketplace rules you attach to the plan — a recurring revenue stream, not just a vanity badge.

Driver steps

  1. Sign in with a driver account.
  2. Open Subscriptions from the driver menu / settings.
  3. Choose a plan and complete Subscribe through an enabled payment path.
  4. Confirm the plan shows as active and that any access benefits apply on the next shift.

Operator setup

Create and edit driver tiers in admin → Subscriptions (target = driver). See Admin subscriptions.
