# Surge pricing — Customer app

Delivery fee chip: standard vs surge multiplier when demand exceeds courier capacity.

## Demo

1. Few drivers online + several open delivery orders.
2. Customer restaurant / checkout — elevated fee or surge label.
3. Bring drivers online / let demand fall — fee returns toward standard.
