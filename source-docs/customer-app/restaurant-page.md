# Restaurant details — Customer app

Open/closed, ETA chip, delivery fee / surge chip, restaurant offers and reviews.

## Smoke test

1. From [discovery](./discovery.md), open a restaurant.
2. Confirm **open/closed** matches restaurant hours ([restaurant hours](../restaurant-app/hours.md)).
3. Note **ETA** chip — see [smart ETA](./smart-eta.md).
4. Note **fee / surge** chip — see [surge pricing](./delivery-fee.md).
5. Scroll **Available Offers** / reviews on that restaurant (not the global Offers tab).
