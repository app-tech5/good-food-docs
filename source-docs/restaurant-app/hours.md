# Hours & delivery zone — Restaurant app

Opening hours, prep time, and delivery radius that drive open/closed and job reach.

## Steps

1. Open hours / delivery settings in the restaurant app.
2. Set weekly hours; save before service.
3. Review radius / prep time — affects customer open/closed and driver assignment distance.
4. Customer restaurant page should show matching open/closed ([restaurant details](../customer-app/restaurant-page.md)).
