# On the road — Driver app

Active job controls and customer / address details while delivering.

## Steps

1. From [deliveries](./deliveries.md), open the active job.
2. Confirm status actions (navigate, arrived, complete).
3. Open details — customer phone, address notes, order lines.
4. Keep location on so customer [live tracking](../customer-app/order-tracking.md) updates.
5. Finish with [proof of delivery](./proof-of-delivery.md) when required.
