# Languages & RTL — Driver app

Change EN / FR / ES / AR from driver Settings (RTL for Arabic).

## Steps

1. Driver → **Settings** → language.
2. Confirm Deliveries / Active job strings update.
3. Try Arabic RTL on the job board.
