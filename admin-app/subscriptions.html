Subscription plans

Source of truth for plans sold in the customer, driver, and restaurant apps.

Tiers Targets Benefits

Subscription tiers for every role

Each plan has a target: customer, driver, or restaurant — plus price, billing cycle, active flag, and benefit text / flags.

Create or edit a plan

  1. Open Subscriptions in the admin sidebar.
  2. Review existing rows (name, target, price, cycle, active).
  3. Add New or View to edit: set target audience, price, billing cycle, and benefits.
  4. Save, then open the matching mobile app and confirm the plan appears.
