Admin app

The operator console — configure the city, watch money and partners, and support incidents without chasing three mobile screenshots.

Web Control room Marketplace rules

Who this app is for

Platform operators, city managers, and support leads. They decide which restaurants go live, which gateways take money, which languages and taxes apply, and how to investigate a stuck order before the customer churns.

It is desktop-first on purpose: wide tables and filters beat a phone UI when you are comparing fifty restaurants or a week of sales.

Where it sits in the suite

Admin is not a fifth marketplace — it is the cockpit for the other four:

  • Seed and govern — users, restaurants, drivers, catalog, settings
  • Monetize — commissions, plans, gateways, sponsored oversight
  • Grow — promotions and coupons customers redeem in the customer app
  • Prove — reports and the money ledger when finance asks “why?”

Change a restaurant here and the customer feed updates. Cancel an order and the driver is freed. That only works because admin writes to the same API the mobiles read.

What “good” looks like for operators

Morning: dashboard pulse. Incident: orders list with full detail. Launch in a new city: languages, currency, taxes, fees, and COD aligned. End of week: sales and partner reports instead of a spreadsheet archaeology project.
