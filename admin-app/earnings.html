Commissions and earnings

Every completed order splits value between the marketplace and the restaurant. The baseline platform commission is configured in marketplace / app settings; restaurant subscription benefits can soften that cut.

The Earnings area is where you review periods and totals before payouts or investigations — first as a list of earning records, then as a detail form when you need the full split math.

Earnings list

Open Earnings to scan periods, restaurants, and totals at a glance. Use filters when you investigate a single partner or a date range.

  1. Set or review the default commission rate in App Settings.
  2. Place a test order end-to-end (customer → restaurant accept → complete).
  3. Open Earnings and confirm platform vs restaurant totals appear for that activity.

Earnings details

Click View on a row to open the earning record for a period. The detail screen starts with an Earnings summary (period range, reference id, and total), then breaks that total into platform commission, restaurant earnings, delivery earnings, and taxes.

Below the summary you get the contributing Transactions (date, restaurant, amount, commission, delivery, status) and any related Payouts (recipient, type, amount, pending vs completed). Use this when a partner disputes a payout, when you verify a subscription changed the cut, or when support needs the exact numbers behind a period total.

  1. From the Earnings list, open View on the period you care about.
  2. Confirm the summary split (platform / restaurant / delivery / taxes) adds up to the total.
  3. Scan Transactions for the restaurants and amounts in that period; check Payouts if money should already be owed out.
  4. If numbers look wrong, cross-check App Settings commission and the related orders.
