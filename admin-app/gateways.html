Payment gateways

Gateways tell the platform which PSPs and local methods are available. Turn on only what you have credentials and compliance for.

Configure for a market

  1. Open Gateways in admin.
  2. Enable the methods you support; disable the rest.
  3. Enter provider credentials / fees as your form requires.
  4. Verify a test checkout and a wallet top-up from the customer wallet path.
