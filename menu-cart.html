Overview Getting Started

Menu & cart

Browse the catalog, add items with quantities, then review the cart before checkout.

Menu Add to cart Cart

Build the basket

Once a restaurant is open, conversion is catalog → add → cart. Each screen below is a distinct moment.

Menu — categories and items

The menu lists categories with dish name, short description, price, and a + control. This capture is the catalog itself — not status chips or the cart banner.

  1. Scroll categories until you find the dish.
  2. Tap + on an item (or open the item if your flow uses a detail sheet).

Add to cart — quantities and View Cart

After an item is added, the menu shows quantity steppers (− / +) and a floating View Cart bar (item count + total). The tab-bar cart badge increments too.

Cart — line items and totals

The cart lists quantities, extras, and a running total. Change amounts or remove lines here before checkout.

  1. Open the cart from the tab bar or the View Cart bar.
  2. Review quantities, extras, and totals.
  3. Continue to checkout when the basket looks right.
