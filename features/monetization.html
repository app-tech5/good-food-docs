Monetization

Turn every order into revenue — and keep earning between deliveries with plans, ads, and wallet.

Commissions Subscriptions Sponsored ads Wallet & payments

Your marketplace, your money

Good Food Pro is built for operators who want more than a simple delivery fee. You take a platform cut on orders, sell membership plans, let restaurants buy premium placement, and offer wallet balances customers love to top up.

Everything is managed from the admin console and the apps your partners already use — so monetization feels like part of the product, not a bolt-on spreadsheet.

Commissions on every order

Set a default commission for your city, or tune it per restaurant. When a partner pays for a stronger subscription, you can reward them with a lower cut — a clean incentive that grows both sides of the marketplace.

Earnings views in admin show the split clearly: what the platform keeps, what the restaurant earns, ready for payouts.

Subscriptions that renew

Offer monthly (or other) plans to customers (free delivery, member offers), drivers (access and support tiers), and restaurants (better rates, visibility). Recurring revenue sits next to commission income — the combination buyers look for in a serious food platform.

Sponsored listings

Restaurants compete for attention on home and search with paid campaigns: set a headline, schedule, budget, and watch impressions and clicks. Your customer app stays beautiful; partners pay for the spotlight.

Wallet & modern payments

Customers can keep a balance, enjoy cashback-style rewards, and get smoother refunds. Checkout supports major cards via Stripe Connect–style marketplace payouts and regional payment providers so you can launch in more countries with the payment habits locals already trust.

Why this converts

Buyers evaluating food-delivery clones often ask: “How do I make money after launch?” Good Food Pro answers with a full toolbox — commissions, subscriptions, ads, wallet, and gateways — included in the same five-product suite you host and brand.
