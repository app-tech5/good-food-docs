Monetization

A food marketplace only works if money flows the right way — on every order, every month, and every time a restaurant wants more visibility. This suite gives you several ways to earn, side by side, without bolting on a separate billing product.

Commissions Subscriptions Sponsored ads Wallet & payments

Earn on the order — and between orders

Most food apps stop at “take a cut of the basket.” That is useful, but it is not enough when you want a business that survives quiet weekday afternoons. Here you can combine a platform commission with renewing membership plans, paid placement for restaurants, a customer wallet that encourages top-ups, and payment options that match the countries you serve.

Operators manage the levers from the admin console; customers, drivers, and restaurants see the matching screens in their apps.

Commissions that stay flexible

Set a default commission for your market, then adjust it per restaurant when you need to. A new partner might need a gentler start; a high-volume kitchen might accept a standard rate. When a restaurant upgrades to a stronger subscription, you can reward them with a lower cut — a clear incentive that grows both sides of the marketplace.

Admin earnings show the split: what the platform keeps, what the restaurant earns, ready for payouts.

Subscriptions that renew on their own

Offer plans to customers (free delivery, member offers), drivers (access tiers), and restaurants (better rates, visibility). You configure tiers in admin; each app shows its own subscribe screen. Recurring revenue sits next to commission income — even when order volume dips.

Admin — subscription tiers for every role

Sponsored listings restaurants will buy

Restaurants bid for search or home placement with a headline, budget, and schedule. Your customer feed stays polished; partners pay for the spotlight.

Restaurant — launch a sponsored campaign

Wallet, cashback, and payments that travel

Customers keep a balance, top up when they need to, and move through refunds with less friction. Gateways in admin help you match the payment habits of each market (cards and regional PSPs).

Customer wallet

Why buyers care

A pretty menu browser is easy to demo. A platform that can commission, subscribe, advertise, wallet, and settle — in the same five-product suite you host and brand — is what turns a trial install into a real operator story.
