Monetization

A food marketplace only works if money flows the right way — on every order, every month, and every time a restaurant wants more visibility. This suite gives you several ways to earn, side by side, without bolting on a separate billing product.

Commissions Subscriptions Sponsored ads Wallet & payments

Earn on the order — and between orders

Most food apps stop at “take a cut of the basket.” That helps on busy nights, but a real operator needs more: commissions on orders, membership plans that renew, paid placement restaurants will buy, a wallet that brings customers back, and payment options that match each market.

You configure the rules in admin; customers, drivers, and restaurants see the matching screens in their apps — so monetization feels native, not like a spreadsheet on the side.

Admin — subscription tiers you sell across the marketplace

Commissions that stay flexible

Set a default commission for your city, then tune it per restaurant. A new partner might need a gentler start; a high-volume kitchen can keep the standard rate. When a restaurant upgrades to a stronger plan, you can reward them with a lower cut — an incentive that grows both sides of the marketplace.

Earnings views show the split: what the platform keeps, what the restaurant earns, ready for payouts and reporting.

Admin earnings — platform vs restaurant split

Subscriptions that renew on their own

Offer plans to customers (free delivery, member offers), drivers (access / priority tiers), and restaurants (better rates, visibility, SaaS-style access). You create tiers in admin (target, price, billing cycle, benefits); each app shows its own subscribe screen.

Recurring revenue sits next to commission income — still paying when order volume dips.

Sponsored listings restaurants will buy

Attention on home and search is limited. Partners create a campaign: headline, daily bid, placement (search top, home banner, or both), then launch. Your customer feed stays polished; restaurants pay for the spotlight.

Restaurant — launch a sponsored campaign

Wallet, cashback, and payments that travel

Customers keep a balance, top up when they need to, and store cards under payment methods. Cashback-style rewards and smoother refunds (when enabled) make the wallet useful day to day. Gateways in admin help you match cards and regional PSPs to each market.

Why this converts

Buyers often ask: “How do I make money after launch?” Commissions, renewing plans, sponsored ads, and wallet — in the same suite you host and brand — is a stronger answer than a pretty menu browser alone.
