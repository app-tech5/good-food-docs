Kitchen Display (KDS)

A clear kitchen board for busy stations — tickets that move as fast as the cooks.

Restaurant app Live tickets Paperless prep

Built for the line, not the office

When orders pile up, staff need a board they can read at a glance — not a cluttered chat of notifications. The Kitchen Display in the restaurant app turns accepted orders into prep tickets your team can advance through the kitchen.

From customer tap to plate

A customer orders → the restaurant accepts → the ticket appears on the KDS → the kitchen marks progress → the driver is ready to pick up. Everyone shares one order story: admin sees earnings, drivers see the route, cooks see the food.

Ready for demos and go-live

Sample kitchen tickets are included in the demo data so you can show the board on day one — perfect for investor walkthroughs, partner onboarding, and CodeCanyon previews.

Why buyers care

Paperless kitchens reduce mistakes and speed service. Bundling KDS inside the restaurant app means you sell a complete partner toolkit — orders, menu, subscriptions, sponsored ads, and the kitchen board — without a separate expensive add-on.
