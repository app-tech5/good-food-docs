Logistics & proof of delivery

Batch nearby jobs, live map tracking, and multimedia proof of delivery for the last mile.

Batching Live map POD + signature

Order batching

Drivers can pick up compatible nearby jobs. Backend logistics helpers (findBatchCandidates (and related limit / radius rules from delivery settings) live in logisticsService, exposed under /api/logistics.

Live map tracking

Customer and driver apps share live location / map UX so buyers follow the courier and drivers navigate to drop-off. Restaurant and admin surfaces remain order-centric; tracking is a first-class last-mile feature, not a decorative map widget.

Proof of delivery (POD)

The delivery app supports multimedia confirmation at drop-off:

  • ProofOfDeliveryModal — capture / attach delivery proof
  • SignaturePad — customer signature
  • Wired through delivery action hooks (useDeliveryActions / driver order hooks)

This closes the loop for disputed deliveries and COD / high-value drops.

  • my-backend/src/routes/logisticsRoutes.js → /api/logistics
