Logistics & proof of delivery

Smarter routes for drivers, live tracking for customers, and proof when it matters.

Batching Live map Photo + signature

Batch nearby jobs

Couriers can take compatible deliveries that sit close together — fewer empty miles, more completed orders per shift. Your marketplace stays efficient even when demand spikes at lunch and dinner.

Follow the driver live

Customers watch their order move on the map. Drivers navigate to the door with confidence. That transparency cuts support tickets and builds the “wow” moment that makes people order again.

Proof of delivery

At the door, drivers can capture a photo and a customer signature. Perfect for cash-on-delivery, high-value meals, and any dispute where “I never got it” used to be hard to answer.

Last mile that sells the product

Serious buyers compare clones on logistics depth. Batching, live tracking, and multimedia proof show that Good Food Pro is ready for real city operations — not a demo that stops at “order placed.”
