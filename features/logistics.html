Logistics & proof of delivery

The last mile is where trust is won or lost. Batching nearby jobs, live status on the way, and proof at the door turn “order placed” into an operation your city can actually run.

Batching Live tracking Photo + signature

Fewer empty miles, more completed drops

When lunch peaks, sending a courier across town for a single bag burns time and fuel. Batching surfaces compatible jobs that sit close together so drivers stack drops intelligently. Assignment radius and delivery settings stay under your control — dense downtown fleets and spread-out suburbs should not share one unrealistic rule.

Follow the order to the door

Customers watch status move from pending to preparing, out for delivery, and delivered — with ETA when available. That transparency cuts “where is my food?” tickets and creates the small wow moment that makes someone order again tomorrow. Drivers stay oriented with clear pickup and drop-off details on their side of the suite.

Customer — track order status live

Proof when “I never got it” used to win

At the door, drivers can capture a photo and a customer signature. Cash-on-delivery, high-value meals, gated buildings, and neighbour handoffs suddenly have evidence. Disputes become conversations with context instead of he-said-she-said that burns support time and partner trust.

Pair with Kitchen Display for kitchen-to-door, and Monetization when driver subscription tiers are part of your courier program.
