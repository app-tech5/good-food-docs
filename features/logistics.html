Logistics & proof of delivery

The last mile is where trust is won or lost. Batching nearby jobs, live map tracking, and proof at the door turn “order placed” into an operation your city can actually run.

Batching Live map Photo + signature

Fewer empty miles, more completed drops

When lunch peaks, sending a courier across town for a single bag burns time and fuel. Batching surfaces compatible jobs that sit close together so drivers stack drops intelligently. Assignment radius stays under your control.

Follow the bag on the map

Customers watch the courier approach. Drivers navigate with confidence. That transparency cuts “where is my food?” tickets and creates the wow moment that makes someone order again.

Customer — live order tracking

Proof when “I never got it” used to win

At the door, drivers can capture a photo and a customer signature. Cash-on-delivery, high-value meals, and gated buildings suddenly have evidence — disputes become conversations with context.

Last mile that sells the whole suite

Pair with Kitchen Display for the kitchen-to-door story, and Monetization when driver subscription tiers are part of your courier program.
