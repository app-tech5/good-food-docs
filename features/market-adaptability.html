Market adaptability

Launch in more cities and cultures — languages, money, and payment habits included.

EN · FR · ES · AR Multi-currency Local payments Extra channels

One product, many markets

Food delivery is local. Good Food Pro is ready to feel local too: switch the language your customers see, show prices in the currency they expect, and plug in the payment methods that win trust in that country.

Languages people actually use

The suite ships with English, French, Spanish, and Arabic (Arabic is right-to-left). Users can change language in settings; your brand stays consistent across customer, driver, restaurant, and admin.

Need another language later? The same translation workflow used for these four applies — see Environment setup — Languages.

Currencies that match the street

Configure the currency your apps display and that admin reports use. Operators running multi-city demos or regional rollouts can keep finance readable for staff and customers alike.

Reach beyond the app store

Hybrid channels let you open the door for customers who discover you on messaging or other entry points — while every order still flows into the same kitchen, driver, and earnings pipeline.

Payments that fit the region

Pair global card rails with regional processors where your buyers already pay. Wallet cashback and fast refunds keep the experience modern and sticky after the first order.
