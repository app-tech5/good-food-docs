Market adaptability

Food delivery is local. Language, currency, how people pay, and how orders arrive at the kitchen all change from city to city. This platform is built so you can feel local without rewriting the product for every launch.

Languages Currencies Channels Regional payments

Speak the way your city speaks

Customers, drivers, restaurants, admin, and the API all ship with English, French, Spanish, and Arabic — including right-to-left layout where Arabic needs it. People switch language in settings; from admin you control which languages are active and which is the default.

Language picker in the customer app

Price in the currency people expect

Display and reporting follow the currency you configure for the market. Menus, carts, wallets, and admin reports stay aligned — so partners and customers always see prices that make sense locally.

Hybrid channels, one order brain

Not every order starts in your mobile app. Counter, phone, or partner channels can still land in the same kitchen and courier flow — one order pipeline for status, earnings, and logistics.

Payments and wallet habits that fit the region

Card rails, regional PSPs, and wallet behaviours are part of feeling local. See also Monetization for commissions, plans, ads, and gateways.
