Market adaptability

Food delivery is local. Language, currency, how people pay, and how orders arrive at the kitchen change from city to city. This platform is built so you can feel local without rewriting the product for every launch.

Languages Currencies Channels Regional payments

Speak the way your city speaks

Customers, drivers, restaurants, admin, and the API ship with English, French, Spanish, and Arabic — including right-to-left layout where Arabic needs it. People switch language in settings; you are not stuck shipping an English-only demo to a bilingual market and apologizing later.

From admin you enable languages and pick the default. On phones, the UI follows the user’s choice so screenshots, support chats, and first-run demos look native to each audience.

Customer settings — pick a language

Price in the currency people expect

Menus, carts, wallets, and admin reports follow the currency you configure for the market. Change the default when you expand; refresh the apps and the numbers tell the right story — no more confusing a partner with a currency they never use at the till.

Hybrid channels, one order brain

Not every order starts in your mobile app. Some markets need counter, phone, or partner channels that still land in the same kitchen and courier flow. Channel configuration describes how demand arrives; the shared order pipeline keeps status, earnings, and logistics consistent — so you do not run two disconnected systems.

Payments and wallet habits that fit the region

Card rails, regional PSPs, and wallet behaviours (cashback, smoother refunds) are part of feeling local. Pair this page with Monetization for the full money story: commissions, plans, sponsored listings, wallet, and gateways together.
