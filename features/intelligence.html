AI intelligence

Smarter carts, clearer arrival times, and fees that flex when the city gets hungry. Built-in intelligence makes every session feel personal — and every peak hour a little more under control.

Recommendations Smart ETA Surge pricing

Intelligence that grows the order

A static menu browser leaves money on the table. Recommendations surface sides, drinks, and extras that fit what people already ordered, the time of day, and even the weather outside. The next add-to-cart feels natural — not like a popup from 2014.

Higher average order value means more commission for you and happier restaurants, without hiring a merchandising team on week one.

Arrival times customers can trust

Arrival times that reduce “where is my food?”

Smart ETA looks at how busy the kitchen is and how long the road will take. Clearer times mean fewer anxious support messages and a calmer inbox when lunch rushes. Customers plan their evening; you look professional instead of guessing “30–45 min” forever.

Surge when demand outruns the fleet

When everyone orders at once and drivers are scarce, delivery fees can flex automatically. You protect service quality in peak hours and capture the value of urgency — the same pattern people already understand from major apps. Quiet hours stay friendly; spikes stay fair.

A modern marketplace story for demos and listings

Recommendations, ETA, and surge help your product story stand out: you are selling an operator-ready platform, not a brochure of static screens. Weather and routing helpers degrade gracefully when the network is imperfect — so demos still work when the café Wi‑Fi is moody.
