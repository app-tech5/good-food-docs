AI / ops intelligence

Built-in recommendations, smart ETA, and surge pricing powered by live signals: order history, kitchen load, traffic, and weather.

Recommendations Smart ETA Surge fee

Overview

The backend intelligenceService powers personalization and ops signals at /api/intelligence — owned by you, running on your stack, with weather and routing inputs (Open-Meteo / OSRM) when available.

Recommendations (cross-sell)

Surface complementary products from order history, time-of-day, and weather context to grow average order value automatically.

Smart ETA

ETA blends kitchen load with travel and rush-hour estimates so customers get sharper arrival times and fewer “where is my food?” moments.

Surge delivery fee

When demand rises against available drivers (and weather pressure when relevant), surge pricing lifts the delivery fee in real time — configured on the server and reflected instantly in checkout.
