AI intelligence

Smarter carts, sharper arrival times, and pricing that flexes with demand.

Recommendations Smart ETA Surge pricing

Intelligence that grows the order

Good Food Pro includes built-in smarts that make every session feel personal. Suggestions learn from what people ordered before, the time of day, and even the weather — so the next add-to-cart feels natural, not pushy.

Recommendations that lift basket size

Show the right sides, drinks, and extras at the right moment. Higher average order value means more commission for you and happier restaurants — without hiring a merchandising team on day one.

Arrival times customers trust

Smart ETA looks at how busy the kitchen is and how long the road will take. Clearer times mean fewer “where is my food?” messages and a calmer support inbox.

Surge when the city gets hungry

When demand rises and drivers are scarce, delivery fees can flex automatically. You protect service quality in peak hours and capture the value of urgency — the same pattern customers already understand from major apps.

A modern marketplace story

AI features help your CodeCanyon listing and sales demos stand out: you are selling an operator-ready platform with recommendations, ETA, and surge — not a static menu browser.
