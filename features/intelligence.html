AI / ops intelligence

Built-in recommendations, smart ETA, and surge pricing — heuristics with live signals (history, kitchen load, traffic, weather).

Recommendations Smart ETA Surge fee

Overview

The backend intelligenceService powers personalization and ops signals exposed at /api/intelligence. It is not a third-party “AI SaaS” dependency: logic runs on your server with optional Open-Meteo weather and OSRM routing, plus safe fallbacks when offline.

Recommendations (cross-sell)

Suggest complementary products using order history, time-of-day buckets, and weather tags so carts grow without manual merchandising rules alone.

Smart ETA

ETA combines kitchen load (active / kitchen statuses) with travel estimates (and rush-hour windows) so customers see more realistic arrival times.

Surge delivery fee

When demand spikes relative to online drivers (and optional weather pressure), a surge multiplier can raise the delivery fee. This is controlled server-side so the customer app stays a thin client.
