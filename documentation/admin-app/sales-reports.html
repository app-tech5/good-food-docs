Sales reports — Admin app

Read-only snapshots of marketplace revenue for a period — either platform-wide or scoped to one restaurant. There isn't much to "configure" here beyond picking what to generate/view; the value is in reading the numbers correctly.

What you're looking at

FieldMeaning
RestaurantThe report's scope — blank/unset means platform-wide across all restaurants
Start Date / End DateThe period the totals below cover
Total SalesGross revenue for the period
Total OrdersOrder count for the period
Average Order ValueTotal Sales ÷ Total Orders
Sales By CategoryRevenue split across menu categories — the bar chart and breakdown table show the same numbers two ways
Delivery FeesDelivery fee revenue collected in the period
Taxes CollectedTax amount collected in the period
Generated ByWhich admin/user triggered the report
Statuspending, completed, or failed — wait for completed before trusting the numbers

How to set it up

  1. Complete some real or demo orders across the period you want to analyze.
  2. Open Sales reports and generate/select a period (platform-wide, or scoped to a restaurant if that's how your build's report generation is triggered).
  3. Wait for Status to reach completed.
  4. Read Total Sales, Orders, Average Order Value for the headline numbers, then check Sales By Category for what's actually driving revenue.
  5. Use this before partner conversations (renewal, commission negotiation); use Earnings instead when you specifically need the platform/restaurant commission split rather than gross revenue.
