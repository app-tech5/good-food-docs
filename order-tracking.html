Order tracking

How customers follow an order from acceptance to delivery, and how that ties to the map and the driver app.

Status steps ETA Map

Follow the order without calling support

After checkout, customers should not need to call support to know where their food is. Track Order is the single place that answers: was it accepted, is the kitchen cooking, is a courier on the way, and when will it arrive.

Status timeline

The screen shows the order id, timestamp, and a vertical status timeline: pending → preparing → out for delivery → delivered. Active steps are highlighted; later steps stay muted until they happen. Restaurant contact details sit on the same screen so the customer can call about the meal itself while delivery progress stays visible — without opening a separate chat.

  1. Place an order and wait for the restaurant to accept it.
  2. Open Order history, select the order, then Track order (or the deep link after payment).
  3. Watch the timeline advance as kitchen and driver statuses change on the shared order record.
  4. When the driver is en route, ETA copy updates when your logistics / intelligence data provides it.

Delivery map while the courier is en route

When the order is out for delivery, Track Order can show a Delivery Map: destination pin, live courier position, route line, and an ETA badge (for example “ETA ~3 min”). That is the live tracking view — not the nearby-restaurant discovery map. Driver-side job handling and proof of delivery are documented under Logistics & POD.
