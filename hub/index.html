The features that close the sale

AI · conversion

Recommendations that grow the cart

Cross-sell from order history, time of day, and live weather — “add a drink” feels personal, not random. Built into the customer app, not a slide.

AI · trust

Smart ETA

Arrival windows from kitchen load, live routing, rush hour, and weather — not a static “30 minutes”.

AI · yield

Surge pricing

Delivery fees rise when demand spikes or drivers are scarce, with a clear surge badge at checkout.

Logistics

Batch nearby jobs. Track the rider live.

When a driver accepts, ready orders nearby can share the route. Customers watch the rider on the map with ETA from kitchen to door.

Proof

Photo + signature POD

Contactless drop-offs require a photo and on-device signature, geofenced to the customer address so disputes close.

Revenue

Three-sided subscriptions

Sell plans to customers (free delivery), restaurants (lower commission), and drivers (priority jobs). You configure them in Admin. They subscribe in each app.

Ads

Sponsored listings

Restaurants bid for search rank and home-banner slots. Paid discovery you inventory from Admin.

Kitchen

Paperless KDS

New / Preparing / Ready columns, large tickets, one-tap status — restaurants ship without paper.

Payments

Stripe Connect, plus the rest of the world

Card checkout and driver payouts on Connect. PayPal, Paystack, Flutterwave, Razorpay, COD, crypto slot, and an internal wallet with cashback and instant refunds.

Markets

WhatsApp, USSD, RTL

Hybrid intake for emerging markets, English / French / Spanish / Arabic RTL, Admin currencies and VAT / GST.

Promos that scale volume

Coupons, flash sales, Buy X Get Y, happy hours — scoped to platform, restaurant, or item.
